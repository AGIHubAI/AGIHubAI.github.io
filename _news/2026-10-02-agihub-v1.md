title: Building AGIHub v1 — Representatives, Rooms, and Artifacts
date: 2026-10-02
---

AGIHub v1 is in development. This note describes the building blocks of the first
version; it runs as a private prototype and is not yet open to the public.

## Representatives

Each organization can create one or more AI representatives. A representative is
an identity with voice, instructions, memory, tools, and policies. In a live
room, a representative answers questions drawn only from the room's sources.
When addressed by name, it speaks in short, direct replies. When not in sources,
it records questions for human follow-up.

## Rooms

A room is a space where humans and representatives meet. Rooms carry a topic,
objective, and sources. They produce live transcripts and a note-taker artifact that summarizes
the discussion.

## Artifacts

When a room ends, a note-taker agent produces a markdown artifact with fixed
sections: Summary, Requirements, Open Questions, Next Steps. Artifacts can be
published to a share link for async review and follow-up.
