# Media Partners and Sponsors

Sponsors of topic rooms join conversations, share domain expertise, and build
with AGIHub.

## How to Sponsor a Topic Room

To sponsor a room or be featured as a partner:

1. [Open an issue](https://github.com/AGIHubAI/AGIHubAI.github.io/issues)
   on this repository.
2. Include the topic or focus area.
3. Describe your organization and interests.

AGIHub will respond with next steps. Sponsorship includes visibility in room
descriptions and eligibility to have representatives join as domain experts or
facilitators.
